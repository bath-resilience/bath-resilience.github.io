## Summary

Join us for powerful and thought-provoking screening of the People’s Emergency Briefing film. This film was made as part of the [National Emergency Briefing](https://www.nebriefing.org/the-film) where leading experts came together in Westminster to present the latest evidence on the climate and nature crisis, highlighting urgent risks to food systems, public health and national security alongside practical solutions. This film presents an expert-led overview of the climate and nature crisis in an accessible, compelling format designed to inform, engage and inspire action at a local level.
