## Summary

This workshop aims to link research communities from across the university of Bath to discuss the resilience of infrastructure systems, with focus on extreme event and implications for climate resilience. A particular focus will be on quantitative models for soil-water interactions across a wide range of temporal and spatial scales. The event will be organised as a series of short talks followed by group aiming to identify shared interests and opportunities for future collaboration.
